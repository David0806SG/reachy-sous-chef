# Pan-Seared Ribeye with Garlic Butter

Serves 2. Total about 25 minutes including resting.

## Ingredients
- 2 ribeye steaks, 2.5 cm thick, at room temperature 30 minutes
- Flaky salt and coarse black pepper
- 1 tbsp neutral oil (rice bran or grapeseed)
- 40 g unsalted butter
- 3 garlic cloves, smashed
- 2 sprigs thyme

## Steps
1. Pat the steaks completely dry. Season both sides generously with salt and pepper.
2. Heat a heavy pan (cast iron ideally) over high heat until it just starts to smoke. Add the oil.
3. Lay the steaks in away from you. Sear 3 minutes without moving for a deep brown crust.
4. Flip. Sear 2 to 3 minutes on the second side.
5. Drop the heat to medium. Add butter, garlic and thyme. Tilt the pan and baste for 1 minute.
6. Check temperature: 52 °C for medium-rare, 57 °C for medium. Pull 2 to 3 degrees early; it carries over.
7. Rest on a warm plate 5 minutes, loosely tented. Slice against the grain and spoon over the pan butter.

## Cues
- Crust ready when the steak releases from the pan on its own.
- Butter should foam, not brown black. If it smokes, lower the heat.
