# Hainanese Chicken Rice

Serves 4. About 1 hour 30 minutes.

## Ingredients
- 1 whole chicken, about 1.4 kg
- 6 slices ginger, 4 spring onions, 2 tsp salt
- Ice bath (large bowl of iced water)
- 2 cups jasmine rice, rinsed
- 3 tbsp chicken fat or oil, 4 cloves garlic minced, 2 tbsp ginger minced, 2 pandan leaves knotted
- Chilli sauce: 6 red chillies, 3 cloves garlic, 2 cm ginger, 2 tbsp lime juice, 1 tsp sugar, 3 tbsp chicken stock
- Ginger paste: 4 tbsp grated ginger, 2 tbsp oil, pinch salt
- Dark soy sauce, cucumber slices, coriander to serve

## Steps
1. Rub the chicken inside and out with salt. Stuff the cavity with ginger and spring onions.
2. Bring a large pot of water to a boil, enough to cover the bird. Lower the chicken in breast-side down.
3. Return to a bare simmer, then cover and cook 40 minutes at the lowest simmer. Do not boil.
4. Lift the chicken out and plunge straight into the ice bath for 10 minutes. Keep the stock.
5. Rice: fry garlic and ginger in chicken fat until fragrant, about 2 minutes. Add rice and stir 1 minute.
6. Add 2 and a half cups of the chicken stock and the pandan leaves. Cook as usual (rice cooker or 15 minutes covered, 10 minutes rest).
7. Blend the chilli sauce ingredients. Mix the ginger paste.
8. Chop the chicken, brush with a little sesame oil and light soy. Serve with rice, sauces, cucumber, coriander and a bowl of the stock.

## Cues
- Chicken is done when a skewer at the thigh runs clear, or thigh reads 74 °C.
- Ice bath is what gives the skin its gelatinous snap. Don't skip it.
